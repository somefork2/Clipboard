Everything you copy, one keystroke away.

CopyWell remembers what you copy on your Mac — text, links, images, files — and hands it back instantly. Press ⌥⌘V anywhere, pick a clip, carry on working.

What it does

  • A palette at your cursor. Search as you type, move with the arrow keys, jump with ⌘1–⌘9, preview with ⌘Y.
  • Finds things you cannot remember. Search runs over text, links, source app, and text recognised inside copied images.
  • Pinboards. File the clips you reuse — signatures, snippets, addresses — and keep them for good.
  • A paste stack. Queue several clips and place them one after another.
  • Right-click menus. CopyWell appears in the Services menu of every app, and as its own menu in Finder.
  • Sync between your Macs. Through your own private iCloud database, if you want it.

What it refuses to do

It never asks for Accessibility access

Most clipboard managers press ⌘V for you, which means asking permission to control your Mac and watch your screen. For an app that already sees everything you copy, that is too much power to hold. CopyWell puts the clip on the clipboard and returns you to your work; you press ⌘V.

It skips your passwords

When 1Password, Bitwarden, or Keychain Access marks a copy confidential, CopyWell does not record it — and neither does it record anything shaped like an API key or a private key.

It sends nothing to anyone

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no server. Your history is a file on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Price

Everything is free for 30 days — no card, no account. After that a CopyWell Pro subscription keeps it running; without one the app locks and stops recording, though nothing you saved is deleted and it all comes back the moment you subscribe. The price in your own currency is shown on the purchase screen inside the app.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
